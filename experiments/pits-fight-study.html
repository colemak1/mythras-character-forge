Fight study: ten beats, broadsword against broadsword

One continuous fight on the same movement as the exchange study: every beat starts from the pose, balance and blade the last one left. The outcomes are scripted animation fixtures, not a rolled or validated Mythras sequence: a parried cut, a slipped counter, a low cut met low, a high parry, a minor wound to A's sword forearm, a deflected thrust, and a cut to B's thigh that ends the fight. It plays straight through; tick "Stop where a decision would be made" to hold at each decision. A prototype only; the live Pits does not use it.
