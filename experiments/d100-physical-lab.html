What makes these different

A: the genuine 100-face model. The centerpiece has 100 numbered facets. A separate physical tens die (00–90) and ones die (0–9) show the percentile breakdown as they settle on the tabletop.

B: the recognizable 20-face model. Its centerpiece is still only a d20-shaped object, but its two true d10 percentile dice show the same rolled outcome. The larger number appears above them when the roll resolves.

Both use Three.js and cannon-es for 3D rendering and rigid-body collisions. The fair d100 value is sampled before animation; the dice finish oriented to their assigned faces. For percentile dice, 00 + 0 = 100, 00 + 7 = 7, and 30 + 0 = 30. Drag the scene to inspect. WebGL and internet access for the libraries are required. Practice Combat is unchanged.
