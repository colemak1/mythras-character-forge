What makes these different

A: 100 genuine numbered faces. The shape is built from 98 flat quadrilateral faces and two flat caps. A fair 1–100 value is sampled first, then the physical tumble is guided to land on its corresponding face. The animation is not the random-number generator.

B: physical d20-style body. More readable proportions and pronounced face edges, but only 20 faces. This design displays the full d100 result outside the solid so its face markings never misrepresent the outcome.

Both use Three.js and cannon-es for real-time rendering and rigid-body tabletop collisions. Drag either scene to examine the model. WebGL and an internet connection to load the libraries are required. Neither option changes the actual Practice Combat roller.
