Exchange study: attack, parry, counter, wound, recovery

One exchange, broadsword against dagger. Each body part follows its key poses on its own spring, so the parts arrive at different times. Contacts and blows are constraints and impulses on top of the springs. The results are fixed in advance, not rolled: the cut is parried, the counter wounds the abdomen (a minor wound), both fighters recover. A prototype only; the live Pits does not use it.
