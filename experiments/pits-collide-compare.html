Collision: the same fight, without and with

Both sides play the same fight: the same weapons, the same exchanges and outcomes, written by the same movement. On the right, a blade cannot pass through the other fighter: when one would, the hand is pushed back out (the blade slides, it does not turn), or, if the arm cannot go there, the wrist turns it away. Real contacts (parries, wounds) are left alone. A red ring marks a frame where a blade is inside the other fighter, outside a real contact. The outcomes are random test fights, not rolled Mythras; a prototype only. In the live trial, add &collide=on to the address to try it there.
