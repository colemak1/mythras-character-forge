**Status:** independent **source/code review only**, not a signed-in session or automated pass. **Source of truth:** user's uploaded *Mythras 2e Core Rulebook* (`pdfcoffee.com_mythras-2e-2-pdf-free(20261021-212124).pdf`, locally inspected, **not committed**), printed **pp.86–111**; supporting printed **pp.5, 61–65, 71, 78–80** for rounds/weapon traits, blood loss and Fatigue. ## Executive findings

- **51 concrete arena comparisons** in six groups: **21 `NEEDS CODE`** rows and **1 `NEEDS COPY`** caution, 21 already-correct checks, two explicit decisions and six scope/guidance/test qualifications. `NEEDS CODE` identifies a rule discrepancy or omitted eligible melee path; `PASS` means the code path matches the cited source *as inspected*, **not** that it passed a browser test; `SCOPE/DECISION` marks a consciously simplified gauntlet feature or unresolved interpretation.
- **Prioritize actual misresolution:** unconditional fatal timer for all Major limb wounds (**PIT-W02**), absent ordinary Knockback (**PIT-W09**), missing legal shorter-reach attack against the opposing weapon (**PIT-R02**), and automated Impale embed without attacker choice (**PIT-SE10**).
- **Special Effects inventory:** `SPECIAL_EFFECT_CATALOG` lists all **43** Core names, but `availableEffects()` offers only **29**. Of the **14 never offered**, ranged-only entries should be excluded intentionally (Circumvent Cover, Drop Foe, Duck Back, Marksman, Overpenetration, Pin Down, Rapid Reload, Weapon Malfunction); **Select Target** needs a third person and is outside current two-fighter arena; **Entangle, Flurry, Grip, Kill Silently, Take Weapon** are missing melee-capable branches (some need context/Unarmed/Assassination support). Availability of a name in a catalogue is not implementation.
- **Parry rounding:** printed Core **p.95** says **Half** but does **not specify integer rounding in that table**. `parryAbsorption` currently returns `Math.floor(damage/2)` as *absorbed damage*, so odd 5 blocks 2 and lets 3 through before armour. This is **not proven Core-correct** by the cited text. A defender-favourable rounding convention instead gives `Math.ceil(5/2)=3` blocked and 2 through. Decide explicitly which integer convention the arena adopts, record it in `docs/decisions.md`, and regression-test damage **1, 3, 5** with armour **0 and 1**; do not misrepresent the unresolved half-rounding as a source-verified rule. Secondary Mythras Imperative guides use general round-up conventions, but this user's Core is authoritative and p.95 itself leaves the detail unstated.
- **Known good branches:** absent parry counts as Failure, differential effects precede damage, effects can arise without HP loss, Bleed's Fatigue cadence, critical Maximise weapon-dice/DM separation, most optional Reach transitions, and the five-second-round exertion check.

## Recommended code batches (Claude, in separate PRs)

1. **P0 — numerical/injury validity:** resolve PIT-P01 explicitly; fix PIT-W02 conditional Major limb time limit, PIT-W09 ordinary Knockback (no added initial metre), PIT-SE10 attacker Impale leave-in choice and PIT-SE11 Barbed extraction, PIT-R02 legal weapon targeting at longer Reach. Keep current shared AP/HP/DM/Initiative tests unchanged.
2. **P1 — current melee weapon legality:** PIT-SE01 Entrapping/Entangle, PIT-SE02 Pin Weapon exception, PIT-SE07 Disarm STR restriction, PIT-SE08 Acrobatics Trip, PIT-SE09 Scar Foe timing, PIT-SE17 Stand Fast/Knockback; support shield Bash and worthwhile Unarmed modes by owner priority. Put targeted deterministic tests in `tests/practice-*.cjs` using seeded or injected dice for odd damage, attack/parry differential, severe wounds and range.
3. **P2 — clarity and simulation scope:** fatigue movement caps, Major-limb prone, local injury penalty, injury history for Impale withdrawal, optional Reach toggle or explicit permanent rule, and mark Normal-mode HP reset as a *gauntlet exception*. Preserve ranged, magic, nonhumanoid and third-party bystander decisions as scope exclusions until explicitly in product plan.

### Minimal deterministic regression scenarios

- An incoming **5** damage with attacking **Large** and defending **Medium** (successful) against **0 or 1 armour**: assert the chosen rounded absorbed amount, then actual HP loss. Repeat with full/equal/no reduction; defending grade Success against attacker Failure earns defensive effects but no attack damage.
- Attack with Main Gauche **normal Success**, parry Failure: Pin Weapon eligible by Entrapping; ordinary Dagger non-critical not eligible; then entangle a valid target and track the affected limb/release.
- STR **10** character against target SIZ **21**, Disarm not offered; smaller target retains normal Size-grade resistance; Trip allows Acrobatics.
- Attack deals **14 pre-parry damage** vs SIZ **10**, with or without Bash: automatic Knockback moves **1m**, no extra metre; test Stand Fast and balance checks. Check Pits does not double-count Bash and ordinary Knockback.
- Major limb wound that is **not** severed/punctured/ripped: do not create fatal clock. Same Major wound with GM-confirmed severing: 5×Healing Rate-minute clock. Torso/head uses a different 2×Healing Rate-Combat-Round clock after successful Endurance.
- A successful Impale that penetrates armour: present **leave embedded vs withdraw**; normal extraction halves without DM/armour, Barbed extraction uses full, and any new injury appears in the wound ledger and First Aid candidates.
- A shorter weapon at an opponent's long Reach can legally attack the *weapon* or try Change Range; after closing, the long weapon cannot parry and its hilt hit uses 1d3+1 and correct Reach-step Size reduction.

### Classification and closure boundary

- The Pits currently models **two humanoid melee combatants**, not all of Core's warfare. Ranged-only effects, projectiles/cover, mount/flight, large-monster anatomy, multi-target attacks and Magic need **explicit separate future scope**, not fictitious "passes".
- Normal-mode full HP reset is a roguelite **house exception** to time-based recovery (Core p.80), and should be labeled as such. Hardcore can preserve injuries by design.
- The three in-repository tests described in PR #181 for AP/HP/DM/Initiative and shared melee weapon data do **not** cover these full-procedure cases. No new tests were run in this Chat source review. Once Claude implements targeted patches, perform one owner-only Work walkthrough through natural dice, parry, effect, severe injury and range transition; document actual pass/fail and any setup/cleanup.
- **Keep previously closed Subsystem 5 intact (main Play Mode)**. The Pits procedure remains separately open and the eight-subsystem progress remains **5/8**.

### PIT-P02 — concurrent Initiative (printed Core p.90)

Core distinction: Initiative is rolled at the beginning of the fight and generally retained, not rerolled each Combat Round. A tie is determined from the **total after relevant bonuses/penalties**, not from matching d10 faces. Equal totals **act concurrently**. Core specifies that outcome, not a particular two-player input UI; if the arena serializes choices, the sequencing choice must not confer a mechanical first-strike advantage. Any additional tie-declaration protocol is **arena implementation convention**, not a new Core rule.

### PIT-P07 — shield attacks (printed Core pp.63, 95–96)

Core distinction: Each shield has a **weapon attack line**, not merely parry Size/passive coverage. Shield Bash uses damage **before parry and armour** and moves one metre per **two full points** rolled. Other bludgeoning Bash uses one metre per **three full points**. These use whole pairs/triples; the separate owner ruling to round **half parry damage blocked upward** (PIT-P01) does not convert Bash movement into an automatic rounding-up rule.

### Claude implementation guidance and evidence boundary

- **First**: encode a tie-aware initiative schedule. Do not choose side A when modified scores tie. A 2-fighter concurrent declaration/resolution policy can be UI-specific, but must preserve both fighters' entitled actions and AP-paid reactions. Consider separate test cases for ties at initial roll, later cycle, and later round when the Fatigue initiative penalty changes.
- **Second**: derive shield attack data from the existing **shared WEAPONS entries** (group Shield) instead of duplicating an eighth independent damage table; retain SHIELDS for passive/active defense. A shield attack picks that item's damage, Size, Reach and effects without corrupting the primary weapon, attack style, opponent targeting, inventory or parry state.
- **Third**: verify Bash with fixed damage totals 1 / 5 / 6: shield 0 / 2 / 3m, ordinary bludgeon 0 / 1 / 2m. Check eligibility at SIZ equality and one point over; no Bash+ordinary Knockback double count. Test after successful full parry/armour. Use current owner parry rounding separately.
