## Suggested minimal patch, not a request for automation

Claude can address the **wording-only** findings by editing the existing `pmHazardsHTML()` paragraphs and testing the presence of the important thresholds, page citations and caveats. Do not add new state machines, decrement timers, apply rolled damage automatically, or infer disease/treatment results from character HP. Respect the existing `pmFatiguePanel()` and `pmAdvanceTimeHTML()` calculations.

Suggested concise reference lines (paraphrases, **not quotations from Core**):

> **Acid (p.68):** Weak 1d2/1 round; Strong 1d4/1d2 rounds; Concentrated 1d6/1d3 rounds. Splash stops after its duration; immersion repeats each round. Acid reduces armour AP before location HP. GM tracks contact and corrosive damage.
>
> **Falling (p.78):** ≤1m none; 2–5m 1d6/one location; 6–10m 2d6/two; 11–15m 3d6/three; 16–20m 4d6/four; +5m adds 1d6. Armour does not protect. Smaller SIZ may reduce distance, larger SIZ adds dice. Acrobatics success reduces equivalent drop by 2m and may avoid prone; soft surfaces may halve distance. Moving vehicles use their speed as half the equivalent table height.
>
> **Fire (p.79):** Intensity 1–5: 1d2, 1d4, 1d6, 2d6, 3d6 damage **each round**. Locations: one for 1–2; 1d4+1 for 3–4; all for 5. Ignition times: 1d4, 1d3, 1d2, 1d2 rounds, then instant. Ignited flammable material and skin beneath take damage ignoring armour; GM handles spread and extinguishing.
>
> **Exertion and sleep (pp.78–79):** Light CON hours/Very Easy; Medium CON minutes/Easy; Strenuous CON seconds, rounded to the next round/Standard. Athletics, Brawn or Endurance depending on effort; failure adds Fatigue. Sleep: after 2×CON waking hours, test Endurance; further checks after half CON hours following success, one grade harder.
>
> **Healing (p.80):** Heal at Healing Rate HP/day for Minor, /week for Serious, /month for Major; recalculate period if severity improves. Major requires successful Healing treatment. Strenuous activity reduces effective Healing Rate by 1d3. An untreated non-dismembering Major Wound risks maiming after Healing skill ÷ 20 days. Magic stabilises physical wounds but does not itself cure suffocation or poison.
>
> **Disease and poison (pp.74–75):** GM tracks application, Potency/opposed resistance, onset, duration, named conditions and the specified cure. Resistance can be checked on exposure or onset. An applicable cure can stop new conditions, but earlier harm may remain. The named conditions (such as Fever or Exhaustion) have their own effects.

## What is not a defect or a completion claim

- **Correct as written:** asphyxiation roll/Fatigue thresholds, blood-loss recovery one level/day, failed exertion/sleep checks causing Fatigue, and the basic Major-Wound treatment gate. Already-correct per-cause Fatigue recovery and Advance time controls should not be rewritten.
- **Scope:** pp.81–85 include Passions, survival, traps, visibility and weather. These are inventoried in separate Subsystem 4 case groups and remain **GM-assisted**; they do not need to be inserted wholesale into the Body popup to satisfy the owner's specified topics.
- **Audit status:** source/text review only, **not** a live UI test, automated test pass, or evidence of an incorrect numerical calculation. Subsystem 4 remains **OPEN**, and the eight-subsystem count remains **4/8**. The Core PDF and rendered pages remain outside GitHub.
