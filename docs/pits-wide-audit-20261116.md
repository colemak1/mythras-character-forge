1. **Dropped weapon still controls reach — Severity 2, reproduced.** Set dagger versus shortspear, long engagement, and trigger the real disarmAndScatter action with zero throw distance. The spear is disarmed and canUseWeapon is false, but engagementInfo still assigns it the longer reach. canAttackBody for the dagger remains false and mustTargetWeapon remains true. Compute effective reach from usable equipment for this exchange, account for a remaining usable shield, and recalculate on Disarm, breakage, pickup and Ready. Test that the dagger can target the body after the drop and that picking up and readying restore the appropriate threat. The uploaded Mythras Core, printed p.106, describes the longer weapon's wielder holding the shorter weapon off. The uploaded Mythras Companion, “Weapon Reach & Sweep Attacks” (PDF p.11), explicitly sends adjacent melee combatants back to that Core procedure. See experiments/practice-combat.html, engagementInfo and disarmAndScatter.
2. **Evade advice substantially overstates survival odds — Severity 2, reproduced.** Attack 57 on 70%, defender Evade 60%. The desktop details say 60% chance to get out of the way. Against that roll the engine accepts 01–06 or 58–60, exactly 9%. Choosing Evade also costs 1 AP and leaves the defender prone. Given this ordinary decision, headline “9% to avoid this hit”, show the cost and prone consequence, and keep the skill rating secondary. Use the same opposed comparison and over-100 adjustment as react() for each die face. For parries, show how much a successful block absorbs. See pits-bottom.js, pbDefenceOdds, and practice-combat.html, react. Core printed pp.50 and 103 cover opposed rolls and Evading.
3. **Attack preview offers impossible defences — Severity 2, reproduced.** Give the enemy 0 AP and inspect the Attack detail. It still advertises parry and evade percentages. Its own defence rows disable both for lack of AP. Generate the preview from the same availability checks used to enable decisions. At 0 AP show “They have no AP left to defend.” Also verify a cramped long weapon with a usable shield, a broken weapon and a shield arm out. See pits-bottom.js, pbActionInfo and pbDefences.
4. **Ground attack travels on its knee — Severity 2, frame-sheet observation.** In cine-ground-thrust.jpg the attacker's rear knee travels across the floor during the extension toward contact at 285 ms and retreats during recovery. The contact pose reads clearly. Preserve it and the seated guard, plant the support knee during extension, then let the torso and weapon provide the last distance. For a gap too large for that motion, stage a distinct approach with a hand or foot visibly taking weight. Verify the short- and long-gap cases at normal speed, from both sides, including the next decision pause.
5. **Withdrawal finish has a second visual accent — Severity 3, frame-sheet observation.** In cine-withdraw-finish.jpg the chest fold follows the pull. Try removing the weapon trail during the involuntary fall and letting the wrist lead the blade down. Compare the change at normal speed before adjusting the choreography.
