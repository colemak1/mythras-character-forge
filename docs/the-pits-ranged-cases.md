# The Pits — ranged weapons: Core code cases (2026-11-07)

**Source:** user's privately held *Mythras Core Rulebook* (2e), printed pages cited below; PDF page = printed page + 1. The weapon/Shield and distance tables were checked against rendered pages. All rules paraphrased; do not commit the PDF. **Scope:** one-on-one Pits fight using the existing metre distance; all cases below require new code or a new test of that code. No "already correct" rows. Optional weapon Reach rules remain on. This does **not** reopen Main Play Mode Combat or change project progress (**5/8**).

| Case | Given → required implementation/test | Core page |
|---|---|---|
| PIT-R01 | At exactly each listed Close/Effective/Long boundary, use the **specific ranged item**'s three metre values: Close can allow Choose Location against a stationary/unaware target, Effective adds no *band* penalty, Long halves rolled damage and lowers projectile Force one step; past Long, no attack. Apply Damage Modifier **only** where that weapon's table says Yes. | pp.62, 65, 107 |
| PIT-R02 | Given human target SIZ 11–20 at 20m versus 21m, apply no distance adjustment at 20m and one difficulty step harder at 21m. Use the **distance × target SIZ** table for every shot, separately from the weapon's three range bands; continue its 20m progression. | p.108 |
| PIT-R03 | Given wind/movement/obscuration plus distance, apply the **most severe** applicable ranged situational grade (not their sum), *then* stack the distance/SIZ adjustment. Use the current difficulty-grade engine, never direct arbitrary percentage subtraction. | pp.107–108 |
| PIT-R04 | Given a loaded ranged weapon, allow **Aim** only after spending a **whole Combat Round**, then improve one applicable situational modifier by one grade for the subsequent shot. A second round adds nothing; moving/attacking before release breaks the held aim. Do not erase the separate distance/SIZ adjustment. | p.107 |
| PIT-R05 | Given a projectile weapon and ammunition count 1 or 0, firing spends **one** projectile and makes the weapon unloaded; no ammunition means it cannot load/fire. An unloaded bow/crossbow/sling cannot attack until its own printed **Load turns** have completed; do not interpret Load as free or as full rounds. | pp.62, 65, 109 |
| PIT-R06 | Given a loaded weapon after a shot, Ready/Load spends the required action turns; on an earned **Rapid Reload** effect reduce **the next** load time by one turn per stacked selection, never retroactively. A thrown item within reach requires Ready Weapon (normally one turn); a spare already held off-hand can switch to throwing hand as a free action. | pp.91–92, 99, 109 |
| PIT-R07 | Given a missile attack against a defender with a usable shield, offer a **1-AP shield Parry** using that shield's Ranged Parry **item trait**, then compare projectile **Force** to shield **Size** for damage blocked. A hand weapon, empty hand, and the bow itself do not qualify as missile parries. Do not mistake Ranged Parry for a p.89 Combat Style trait. | pp.63, 91–92, 95, 107 |
| PIT-R08 | Given an aware defender without a usable shield, allow an AP-paid opposed **Evade** instead of Parry; a successful Evade avoids the missile but still leaves a biped **prone**. A failed Evade does not become a free shield parry; respect existing restrictions on evasion and differential Special Effects. | pp.92, 95, 103, 107 |
| PIT-R09 | Given a dagger/hatchet/shortspear/trident with **Thrown** (or a native javelin, dart, sling etc.), make a genuine **ranged** attack with the item's throwing profile, Force, range and permitted effects; do not simply reuse its melee Reach/Size. A style with **Throw Weapons** may throw another nominal melee weapon without a skill penalty, but with its damage halved; do not halve a native thrown profile again. | pp.63, 65, 89, 107 |
| PIT-R10 | After throwing a unique equipped weapon, remove it from that fighter's hand and available immediate attacks/parries—**no duplicate weapon**. A miss leaves it on the arena ground, recoverable only by reaching/readying it; a successful Impale may leave it in the foe and cannot be reclaimed at a distance. Off-hand spare follows R06. | pp.91–92, 97–98, 109 |
| PIT-R11 | Given firing into a **different pair's** melee, use at least Hard against an edge target and Formidable through a group; if a roll fails *only* due to that penalty, determine an adjacent unintended victim, with no Special Effects transferred. In today's **two-fighter** arena there is no third bystander: never conjure or injure one, and do not impose a crowd penalty merely because shooter and target are engaged with each other. | p.108 |
| PIT-R12 | Given a ranged attack that earns a Special Effect, filter by **actual projectile effects** and the p.100 offence/critical/weapon limits: Choose Location normally needs a critical (unless R01's Close exception); Overpenetration needs a critical **and** another target; no melee-only SEs or auto-effects without winning one. Thrown javelin's Pin Weapon against a shield is the stated noncritical exception. | pp.62, 95–100, 107 |
| PIT-R13 | Given a qualifying shot, implement/offer ranged outcomes rather than only naming them: **Drop Foe** needs at least a Minor Wound plus opposed Endurance; **Marksman** shifts the rolled location only to an adjacent valid body region; **Pin Down** opposes Willpower and can prevent return fire next turn even without HP damage; **Rapid Reload** modifies next load (R06). | pp.97–99 |
| PIT-R14 | Given **Circumvent Cover** or **Duck Back**, require actual represented cover (Duck Back additionally needs the shooter already next to it); with no cover state, hide these choices instead of inventing a barrier. **Overpenetration** needs a second physically aligned victim and must not hit an imaginary Pits actor. | pp.96–98 |
| PIT-R15 | Given an arrow/javelin Impale, use its **printed Impale Size** (not firing Force) for the continuing hindrance; the distant shooter cannot pull the projectile out on their next turn. A person at the wound can attempt extraction under the applicable Ready Weapon/Brawn and damage rules. | pp.65, 97–98, 108–109 |
| PIT-R16 | Given 12m separation and a melee-only fighter, use an AP-paid **Move** to change the existing metre distance within that character's allowed movement, then make melee attack legal **only when actually in weapon reach**. Distinguish physical metres from optional **Close/Change Range** within an already established engagement; no free teleport or free attack while crossing 12m. | pp.91–92, 101, 106–107 |
| PIT-R17 | Given a fighter firing while moving, ordinary ranged attacks are not available above walking gait; **Skirmishing** style permits shooting while running, **not** sprinting, and caps the roll by Athletics. Do not convert a movement into an uncharged extra firing action. | pp.89, 108 |

## Deferred weapons — minimum required code cases, **not** part of the ranged PR

**Lance — keep OUT of the current on-foot Pits.** Its Core **Mount** item trait requires a **moving mount**; showing it as an ordinary Very Long melee weapon is wrong.

| Case once a mounted arena exists | Given → required implementation/test | Core page |
|---|---|---|
| PIT-L01 | Lance only attacks while rider is on a **moving** mount. Rider combat roll is capped by Ride unless the style has **Mounted Combat**. Reject the strike when dismounted, mount stopped, or mount missing. | pp.63, 89 |
| PIT-L02 | Charge requires a **full prior round** of running/sprinting approach; apply one grade harder (unless **Beast-back Lancer**), mount's multi-legged Damage Modifier raised **two steps**, and weapon Size +1. Victim can Parry, Evade or counterattack. A **through-contact** pass grants only the attack/reaction and then moves the mount clear of engagement. | pp.89, 102–103 |

**Garrotte — keep OUT of normal face-to-face Pits.** Add only with a deliberate ambush-start setup; do not import supplement-only Strangle effects.

| Case once an ambush opener exists | Given → required implementation/test | Core page |
|---|---|---|
| PIT-G01 | Garrotte can initiate only from a **genuine ambush** (opposed Stealth vs Perception, where relevant), already at **Touch** Reach and using two available hands. If detected / a conventional duel has begun, its **Stealth** item trait forbids the opening attack. Use its printed 1d2 damage, not a fabricated ongoing strangulation attack. | pp.64, 104–105 |
| PIT-G02 | If first attack hits a **surprised** victim, follow surprise's bonus Special Effect and defence/initiative limits. **Kill Silently** is available only to an appropriately **Assassination**-trained style on that **first** attack; it cannot be a free generic garrotte button. | pp.89, 98, 104–105 |
