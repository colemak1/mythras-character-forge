# Magic batch 2 — Animism: code-needed cases

**Source:** *Mythras 2e Core*, printed pp.130–143; indexed PDF p.131 and p.137 checked, earlier source ledger `docs/magic-subsystem-cases.md` AN-B01–B11. **Implementation comparison:** `js/engine.js`, `js/app.js`, `js/play.js` on main (2026-11-09). Bound spirit rank/CHA cap, binding preflight, bound-spirit command costs and acquisition confirmation already exist: **do not re-specify** MAG-N07–N09 or AN-B03/04/06–10.

| ID | Core page | Required code delta and acceptance |
|---|---|---|
| AN-C01 | **p.131** | Offer **Trance** as a separate action, not generic Binding/casting. Depending on recorded animist cult rank, show the **Observe** preparation time: Follower 1 hour, Spirit Worshipper 1 minute, Shaman 1 round, High Shaman 1 Action. Resolve the Trance skill, consume **1 MP**, with a critical shortening preparation one step (not below an Action), and a fumble barring Spirit-Plane entry for one day. A bound-spirit command must not substitute for Trance. **Implemented 2026-11-10 (Claude):** "Roll Trance" on the Play Mode Animism section; a fumble bars it until "Rest one day" (`tests/magic-casting-hurt.cjs`). |
| AN-C02 | **pp.134, 137** | **Summoning**, separate from a bound-spirit command, must require the appropriate summoning context, spirit identity and Binding roll; charge MP according to the spirit's Intensity and resolve summoned-spirit attitude using the Core table on p.137. Present the encounter outcome for GM confirmation; do not auto-mark summoned spirits bound. Verify the p.137 table visually before transcribing every attitude cell. |
| AN-C03 | **pp.135–136** | If the app offers **creating/upgrading a binding vessel**, take the chosen spirit's Intensity and prior vessel level, record the **Experience Roll** cost (new vessel Intensity count, upgrade difference) and normally a week of construction. Never charge the cost just for typing a spirit into the builder; require explicit GM-confirmed completion. |
| AN-C04 | **pp.137–139** | If the app introduces **Spirit Combat resolution**, keep its spiritual MP totals, opposed action outcomes and distinct Spirit Combat effects rather than treating physical HP damage as spirit damage. Current MP field/note is expressly *bookkeeping only*: do not call it a combat engine until every action/effect has a case and a test. |

**Scope/exclusions:** Spirit negotiation and the diverse powers of individual spirits remain GM-resolved when they lack source-checked child cases. No fabricated universal spirit roster. Implement AN-C01 first; AN-C02–04 require fuller source-table and workflow review before coding. Source-verified requirements are **not** assertions of automated acceptance. Progress 5/8.
