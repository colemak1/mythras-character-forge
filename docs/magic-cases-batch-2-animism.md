## A. Spirit access, characteristics and Trance

| ID | Given | Core says (p.N) | Expected app behaviour |
|---|---|---|---|
| AN2-001 | An animist studies the two magical skills of their tradition. | **Core p.130:** Trance permits perception/travel in the Spirit World; Binding is for summoning, controlling and spirit combat. | Keep Trance and Binding separate, with their proper characteristic bases and uses; never substitute one check for another without a specific rule. |
| AN2-002 | A character without Trance tries to observe an unmanifested spirit. | **Core pp.130–131:** Without a supernatural sense it cannot normally perceive Spirit-Plane entities; Trance can initially detect, but not observe, spirit presences at range POW metres. | State the perception limitation; expose a Trance check rather than showing all spirits automatically. |
| AN2-003 | A Follower / Spirit Worshipper / Shaman / High Shaman attempts to observe spirits. | **Core p.131, Trance Preparation Time:** 1 hour / 1 minute / 1 Round / 1 Action, respectively. | Show the rank-specific time or support GM recording of it, not one universal Trance duration. |
| AN2-004 | A Spirit Worshipper wants conversation, a Shaman wants spirit projection, or a High Shaman wants to drag souls. | **Core p.131:** The respective abilities unlock by rank, with their own preparation-time column; all are not available to a Follower. | Preserve rank-specific eligibility and the relevant preparation-time table. |
| AN2-005 | The animist enters Trance and rolls critical. | **Core p.131:** A Trance attempt normally costs 1 MP; critical success shortens required preparation by one time step, minimum one Action. | Prompt/record the roll and cost and apply the time exception; do not treat critical as necessarily free MP. |
| AN2-006 | The animist fumbles a Trance entry roll. | **Core p.131:** They cannot enter the Spirit World for one full day. | Mark the restriction as GM-managed guidance; do not allow an immediate retry to be presented as Core-legal. |
| AN2-007 | Binding 29% is used against a POW 10 spirit. | **Core p.131:** Maximum coercible/bindable/controllable POW is three times Binding's critical range: 29% has critical range 3, so cap POW 9. | Reject POW 10 before a command or binding roll. Allow POW 9 in an otherwise legal scenario. |
| AN2-008 | A spirit is created with Intensity 0 / 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5. | **Core p.133, Spirit Intensity table:** Roll POW 1d6 / 1d6+6 / 1d6+12 / 1d6+18 / 1d6+24 / 1d6+30 respectively; each higher Intensity adds 6 to the POW band. | Offer correct source ranges and preserve generated POW; do not derive POW directly from Intensity alone. |
| AN2-009 | Spirit attributes are calculated. | **Core p.133:** Action Points use INT+POW; initiative bonus uses average INT and CHA; the spirit has POW-based Magic Points and Spirit Damage based on Spectral Combat. | Do not use corporeal DEX-based initiative or CON+DEX-based AP for spirit stats. |
| AN2-010 | A discorporated mortal has no Binding skill and must calculate Spirit Damage. | **Core p.133:** Use half Willpower on the Spirit Damage table instead. | Offer the untrained fallback rather than automatically giving the person Binding. |
| AN2-011 | An ordinary spirit is to be given combat skill ratings. | **Core p.142:** Default Spectral Combat is 50%+POW+CHA; Willpower is 50%+(POW×2). | Generate or display these bases, with independently specified exceptions for individual spirit templates. |

## B. Finding, negotiating, binding and summoning spirits

| ID | Given | Core says (p.N) | Expected app behaviour |
|---|---|---|---|
| AN2-012 | An animist encounters a potentially hostile, neutral or friendly spirit. | **Core pp.134–135:** Spirit attitude and relationships affect negotiation and willingness to cooperate; an acceptable bargain can avoid coercion. | Capture attitude and GM-approved bargain, rather than assuming every successfully contacted spirit is friendly or automatically bound. |
| AN2-013 | A spirit is to be bound after an initial encounter. | **Core p.135:** Binding involves engaging it in Spirit Combat; the animist must win a bargain or exhaust its MP, and the spirit must fit Binding's POW cap. | Require the acquisition/bargain prerequisite as GM-confirmed input; mere possession of the Binding skill does not instantly produce a fetish. |
| AN2-014 | A bound fetish spirit is inside its intact container. | **Core p.135:** It is held in stasis and protected from hostile magic/spirits; it must be called out to use its power. | Distinguish dormant-in-fetish from released-but-still-bound. Do not apply outside damage to the dormant spirit. |
| AN2-015 | A fetish spirit is released to act near its object. | **Core p.135:** It can act up to its POW in metres from the fetish while the binding persists. | Show the distance rule in its reference; moving beyond the limit requires other lawful abilities. |
| AN2-016 | Two animists vie for a fetish and its contained spirit. | **Core p.135:** Opposed Binding rolls decide who commands it until challenged again. | Present the opposed-roll rule and record current controller; do not let both control it simultaneously. |
| AN2-017 | A spirit is bound to a geographical area. | **Core p.135:** It may range no farther than ten times its POW in metres from the binding point. | Record the anchor and radius; do not substitute fetish POW-metres for location bindings. |
| AN2-018 | A spirit is bound into a living creature. | **Core p.135:** This differs from possession; the creature's original soul must be permanently separated, and the bound spirit's benefits/dangers depend on the host. | Label creature binding separately from passive/dominant possession, leaving the GM to confirm the necessary ritual and cost. |
| AN2-019 | An Intensity 3 spirit requires a new binding vessel. | **Core p.136:** Creation costs one Experience Roll per Intensity and normally takes one week. | Provide **3 Experience Rolls** and one week as the source cost; GM can record completion manually. |
| AN2-020 | A fetish binding is upgraded from Intensity 2 to Intensity 3 for the same type of spirit. | **Core p.136:** Pay only the Experience Roll difference (1). A replacement of the same type and Intensity need not cost new Experience Rolls. | Avoid charging the full new-binding cost for a valid upgrade/replacement. |
| AN2-021 | A Follower / Spirit Worshipper / Shaman / High Shaman has a CHA-based bound-spirit limit. | **Core p.136:** Maximum count is one-quarter / one-half / three-quarters / all of CHA; over-capacity frees the strongest bound spirit(s). | Validate count against rank and CHA; record rounding interpretation before enforcing fractional boundaries. |
| AN2-022 | A spirit's binding object is physically destroyed. | **Core p.136:** The spirit immediately becomes unbound, potentially returning to the Spirit World or attacking. | Update bound/unbound status and leave its ensuing actions to GM resolution. |
| AN2-023 | A legal bound-spirit command is issued. | **Core p.136:** It takes one Action and normally 1 MP, with a Binding roll; critical success issues the command at zero MP. | Show Action/MP cost and correct roll, using the critical exception. |
| AN2-024 | A bound-spirit command fails or fumbles. | **Core p.136:** Failure means it ignores the command and no retry before the next Turn; fumble breaks the binding. | Distinguish failed command from escaped spirit, and do not leave a fumbled command marked 'bound'. |
| AN2-025 | A bound spirit is recalled into a fetish. | **Core p.136:** Return costs one AP and no MP; neutral or hostile spirits require a Binding check to comply. | Distinguish return from a new MP-costing command, and prompt the conditionally required roll. |
| AN2-026 | An animist summons a known spirit from a distance. | **Core pp.136–137:** The spirit must have been previously negotiated with or its true name known, Trance establishes contact, and a Binding summons costs MP equal to Intensity. | Require eligibility/range/MP and do not treat this as a standard 1-MP fetish command. |
| AN2-027 | A summoning Binding roll is critical / successful / failed / fumbled. | **Core p.137:** Arrival in 1d3 Rounds / 1d6 minutes / no notice and retry following day / relationship suspended until amends, respectively. | Present four distinct outcomes; a critical does not simply mean instantaneous arrival. |
| AN2-028 | A summoned spirit's attitude determines its response to the command. | **Core p.137, Attitude of a Summoned Spirit:** An opposed result and whether it is allied, neutral or hostile determine service, departure or hostility. | Resolve or show the attitude matrix, rather than treating any successful summons as obedience. |

## C. Spirit Combat and possession

| ID | Given | Core says (p.N) | Expected app behaviour |
|---|---|---|---|
| AN2-029 | An animist and spirit fight in the Spirit World. | **Core p.137:** Use spiritual attributes; a spirit attacks/parries with Spectral Combat, animist with Binding, and an uninitiated opponent relies on Willpower. | Display the correct participating skills and spiritual stats without inventing physical weapon damage. |
| AN2-030 | A spirit attack is not parried. | **Core p.137:** The attacker deals the relevant Spirit Damage roll to the defender's MP under default Spirit Combat. | Provide correct damage and resource targets; do not apply ordinary armour or hit-location HP under the default. |
| AN2-031 | One combatant has no AP to defend. | **Core p.137:** The attacker can make an unopposed Spirit Combat attack, just as in ordinary differential combat. | Treat the attempted attack as unopposed; do not grant a free parry. |
| AN2-032 | A discorporated Trance user flees a Spirit-Plane fight. | **Core p.137:** Escape is an opposed Trance vs Spectral Combat test and replaces attacking; someone without Trance cannot deliberately navigate home. | Support the distinct escape test and its knowledge gate as GM-assisted guidance. |
| AN2-033 | Spirit combat produces one or more levels of success. | **Core pp.137–138:** Spirit Combat uses differential outcomes and eligible Spirit Combat Special Effects. | Offer the spirit-specific list, not the ordinary physical-combat Special Effects wholesale. |
| AN2-034 | Bleed Essence is selected on a qualifying critical. | **Core p.138, Spirit Combat Special Effects:** It causes a psychic leak of 1 MP per cycle until Spirit Combat ends; available offensively or defensively, critical only. | Respect critical-only qualification and MP attrition. |
| AN2-035 | Compel Bargain is chosen after a qualifying offensive outcome. | **Core p.138:** Opposed Influence vs Willpower can force one deed and end combat. | Keep the additional opposition and deed separate from the attacking roll. |
| AN2-036 | Drive Off is chosen against a spirit. | **Core p.138:** The spirit resists the attack roll using Willpower or leaves combat. | Prompt correct opposition and departing outcome. |
| AN2-037 | Leech Strength is selected on a qualifying critical. | **Core p.138:** The attacker recovers the same MP amount inflicted by the successful hit; critical-only offensive effect. | Credit the attacker's MP without applying it as a second strike. |
| AN2-038 | Possess is selected by a spirit that can possess. | **Core p.138:** Spirit-only offensive effect, subject to defender Willpower against the attack. | Reject non-spirit or incapable initiators, and prompt defender resistance. |
| AN2-039 | Psychic Stun is selected. | **Core p.138:** The affected opponent loses their next Turn; available both offensively and defensively. | Record the lost Turn rather than arbitrary Fatigue or HP loss. |
| AN2-040 | Spirit Lance is chosen. | **Core p.138:** Roll Spirit Damage twice and keep the better result. | Use the higher of two Spirit Damage rolls. |
| AN2-041 | Sunder Binding is selected following a spirit fumble. | **Core p.138:** This breaks the spirit's fetish or binding; offensive or defensive, and conditional on spirit fumble. | Enforce the eligibility and release the bound spirit. |
| AN2-042 | Turn Back or Withdraw is chosen as a defensive effect. | **Core p.138:** Turn Back reflects the attacker's Spirit Damage onto itself; Withdraw allows automatic disengagement to body/fetish/Spirit Plane as appropriate. | Provide defensive-only outcomes and their different effects. |
| AN2-043 | Either participant reaches zero MP in default Spirit Combat. | **Core p.138:** Defeat occurs; GM selects a legally available result such as bound, exorcised, possessed, expelled, obligation or dismissed/ignored. | Record a legal defeat consequence, not automatic death or possession in every case. |
| AN2-044 | A spirit has Recurring and is dissipated. | **Core p.138:** It eventually reforms, normally after Intensity days plus MP recovery time, retaining knowledge. | Do not mark it permanently destroyed; provide GM-assisted recovery/delay notes. |
| AN2-045 | Several spirits attack one mortal, or a group flees multiple opponents. | **Core p.139:** Normal AP/unopposed attack consequences apply; escaping multiple opponents requires beating all, whereas a pack member may disengage if an ally covers. | Preserve the multiple-opponent distinction and do not assume one opposed roll suffices against all. |
| AN2-046 | A campaign chooses Tenacity for Spirit Combat. | **Core p.139:** Tenacity is an **optional** separate pool for Spirit Combat damage; it cannot power magic and recovers every 24 hours with rest on the native plane. | Keep default MP-based combat unless GM enables Tenacity; do not conflate it with MP. |
| AN2-047 | A campaign chooses physical-damage Spirit Combat. | **Core pp.139–140:** This is an optional replacement for default MP damage to corporeal victims and has its own location, armour and wound consequences. | Treat as GM-selected alternate mode, not a default side effect of spirit attacks. |
| AN2-048 | An inhabited mortal is dominantly possessed. | **Core p.140:** The possessor controls the body while the host is aware but helpless; possessor uses its own skills and memories, not the host's. | Distinguish dominant possession from a normal control/trait bonus and keep skills separated. |
| AN2-049 | A mortal is passively versus covertly possessed. | **Core p.140:** Passive leaves host in control while the spirit confers effects; covert also hides the possession, requiring opposed Insight/Trance against the spirit's Stealth to detect. | Record possession form and use the correct detection opposition; avoid automatically revealing covert entities. |
| AN2-050 | A possessed character wishes to expel the spirit without help. | **Core p.140:** Victim cannot purge a possessing spirit themselves; another must defeat and exorcise it or use specific expulsion magic. | Do not offer a universal self-dismiss control for hostile possession. |

## D. Distinct spirit abilities and example-type children (initial selection)

| ID | Given | Core says (p.N) | Expected app behaviour |
|---|---|---|---|
| AN2-051 | A spirit uses **Autonomy**. | **Core p.142:** It can range beyond normal binding distance limits and operate as an independent scout or messenger. | Show this as an exception to fetish/radius defaults. |
| AN2-052 | A spirit uses **Bless** to improve an Attribute or Skill. | **Core p.142:** The bonus depends on Intensity and chosen effect; the affected value cannot exceed double its original level. | Enforce or display the doubling ceiling when a GM applies the benefit. |
| AN2-053 | A **Cannibalistic** spirit hits another spirit in combat. | **Core p.143:** It gains MP equal to the MP inflicted, capped at its own POW. | Apply or record transfer, with no over-cap recovery. |
| AN2-054 | A spirit with **Curse** targets Action Points or a skill. | **Core p.143, Curse Spirit Effects:** AP decrease one per 3 full Intensity; a skill worsens one grade per 2 full Intensity. Other curse types use their own table rows. | Use the proper per-effect scaling, not one generic Intensity-to-penalty rule. |
| AN2-055 | A **Discorporate** spirit attacks a mortal soul. | **Core p.143:** Base Discorporate skill 50%+POW+INT; unwilling targets oppose with Willpower; successful resistance protects against that same spirit for one full day. | Use the source skill and 24-hour repeat protection, recorded by GM. |
| AN2-056 | A **Disease** spirit wins the required attack outcome. | **Core p.143:** An appropriate successful Spirit Combat attack can infect; Endurance determines chronic/acute/terminal or carrier results, and the disease's possible conditions are limited by Intensity. | Keep infection, severity and spirit Intensity separate; do not label every contact as automatic infection. |
| AN2-057 | A spirit has **Eternal**. | **Core p.144:** It receives the Recurring trait from p.138. | Use the recurring-spirit consequence rather than permanent destruction. |
| AN2-058 | A **Healing** spirit heals wound damage. | **Core p.144:** Its healing may treat Minor, Serious and Major Wounds (but not replace lost limbs), at 1 spirit MP for each HP healed. | Record the HP restored and spirit MP paid; do not apply Folk Magic Heal's different restriction to this spirit. |
| AN2-059 | A spirit with **Manifestation** changes planes. | **Core p.144:** One Action is required to switch; on the Spirit Plane it is immune to attacks from corporeal foes. | Respect its distinct plane and one-Action transition. |
| AN2-060 | A **Miasma** spirit invokes horror. | **Core p.144:** Radius POW metres, Magnitude twice Intensity; affected targets who cannot block the magic oppose Willpower or flee. | Record eligible area, magnitude and required resistance rather than automatic fear. |
| AN2-061 | A spirit has **Persistent**. | **Core p.145:** It is immune to magical dismissal, not magical damage or Spirit Combat. | Preserve the distinction between dismissal immunity and complete invulnerability. |
| AN2-062 | A spirit uses **Sagacity**. | **Core p.145:** The spirit may possess an appropriate skill at 100%+(INT×2). | Display skill-specific source rating rather than using the general skill base. |
| AN2-063 | A **Spellcasting** spirit casts on its summoner's behalf. | **Core p.145:** Skill is 50%+POW+CHA, or the spirit may share knowledge with a fetish holder. | Distinguish an actual spirit cast from teaching/share of spells. |
| AN2-064 | A spirit **Subjugates** a weaker spirit. | **Core p.145:** It binds spirits of lower Intensity without fetishes; each subordinate commits MP equal to its Intensity; subjugation ends if the holder falls to 0 MP. | Track Intensity eligibility and commitment as GM-managed; do not use normal fetish-limit rules as the only test. |
| AN2-065 | A spirit uses **Warding**. | **Core p.145:** It blocks incoming magic of Magnitude up to twice the spirit's Intensity, without dispelling the blocked magic. | Check magnitude against the correct threshold, but keep warding distinct from spell dismissal. |
| AN2-066 | A spirit uses **Wither** against a corporeal target. | **Core p.145:** Spirit Damage becomes physical injury bypassing ordinary armour and nonmagical parries; the victim can Evade or flee. | Use the exceptional physical-damage resolution rather than default MP-only Spirit Combat damage. |
