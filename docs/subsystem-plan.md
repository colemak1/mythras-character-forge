# Mythras Forge Core verification plan

Progress: **2/8 subsystems finished**. Numbering is stable; partial work does not count as finished. A subsystem is finished only when its applicable Core rules and exceptions are inventoried, implementation or explicit GM handling is checked against the user's uploaded Core rulebook, meaningful automated cases pass, and relevant signed-in workflows are verified on the deployed revision. Open interpretations, unsupported behavior, and exclusions must be explicit. This is not a book-wide accuracy percentage.

1. **Character creation and background** — characteristics, attributes, cultures, community, passions, careers, age, bonus skills and creation flow. Complete for the Ordinary human Core Full Creation scope, with explicit GM-assisted handling and documented interpretations. See character-creation-completion.md for source, automated and signed-in deployment evidence. Other seven subsystems and supplemental workflows are not certified by this completion.
2. **Skills and task resolution** — standard/professional skills, difficulty, augmentation, caps, opposed/differential and group rolls, and task rounds. First pass checked Core pp. 37–38, 50–52 and 65–67, fixed an Automatic-grade regression, and exposed GM adjudication guidance in Play Mode. All 58 base skill formulas match p.53. Spoken fluency and the optional simplified modifiers have been checked against pp.38, 42, 47 and 51. The cap and augmentation calculator is covered by regression tests, and contextual outcomes are documented as GM adjudication or assigned to their dedicated subsystem. Signed-in verification passed in Play Mode and the Sheet for caps, augmentation, contested rolls, fluency, and Automatic checks. Automatic with an Advantage was covered locally. See ../CHANGELOG-SKILLS-TASK-AUDIT.md.
3. **Equipment and economy** — money, starting gear, armour, weapons, encumbrance and their entries. Starting money, social class, and base armour checked against Core pp. 23–24, 34, 58 and 77. Personal weapon and shield tables checked against pp. 63–65. GM-approved material overrides apply to ENC and initiative. Signed-in verification and the remaining equipment procedures are open.
4. **Advancement, hazards and recovery** — improvement, training, fatigue, injuries, healing, ageing, poison and other hazards. Partial audit.
5. **Combat** — actions, styles, rounds, attacks, defences, effects and damage interactions. Partial audit.
6. **Magic and organizations** — all Core traditions, spells, spirits, cults, ranks and interactions. Partial audit.
7. **Creatures and GM procedures** — creature traits and statistics, GM systems and applicable optional rules. Inventory incomplete.
8. **Campaign and live integration** — campaign configuration, permissions, GM/player workflows, persistence, imports and deployment verification. Partial live evidence; incomplete.

The guided combat practice tutorial is a later feature, after the builder is verified and corrected; it is not included in the finished count. New work outside these subsystems must be identified explicitly. Detailed source and case tracking remains in `core-rule-scope.md` and linked audit files.
