## How it feels

- **What works.** The parchment continent makes me want to pick somewhere and go. The strongest moment is landing in Shamur and immediately seeing a whole walled city, with enough street detail to start imagining where a session could happen. The grey space gives the nation map room to move, and the breadcrumb makes going deeper feel low-risk.
- **What breaks the spell.** The continent, Valeshar and Shamur use three visibly different palettes and label styles. Shamur even has its own generator menu in the corner. The clouds soften the change, but the landing still feels like switching maps. On the wheel dive, a frame of the outgoing map showed the coast while the sidebar already said Shamur.
- **First polish pass. Severity 2, workable.** Give the capital one persistent on-map label. The plate says Golshehr until hover, and the live Shamur label can overlap it. A player should be able to find the campaign capital and tell that it opens without checking the sidebar. After that, I’d give the three maps a shared visual treatment before making the clouds thicker or slower.
